Our story

Built to turn beginners into musicians

Wattar Academy was founded with a simple mission: to make quality music education accessible to everyone. We believe that music is a universal language that connects people and enriches lives.

Our structured 4-year curriculum takes students from their very first note to confident performance, with personalized attention and professional guidance at every step.

How we teach

The Wattar method

Every student moves through the same clear cycle, level after level.

01

Find your level

A first session with a trainer to see where you are and which level to start from.

02

Learn in levels

Each level is 4 sessions, with practice assignments waiting for you in the student portal.

03

Evaluate & move up

Your trainer evaluates every level before you move on, so progress is real and measurable.

04

Perform

Recitals, the Wattar Band and the graduation recital put your skills on stage.
