The journey

4 years. 48 levels. One musician.

A progressive path from beginner to advanced musician.

01
Year 1

Foundation

Core skills, proper technique and music reading.

02
Year 2

Development

Wider repertoire, new styles and ensemble playing.

03

Refinement

Advanced technique, expression and performance prep.

04
