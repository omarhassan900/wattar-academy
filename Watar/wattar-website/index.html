The journey

One clear path, from first note to the stage.

Each level is four sessions. Your trainer evaluates it before you move on, so parents always know where their child stands.

  1. Year 1Levels 1–12

    Foundation

    Technique, rhythm and reading music.

  2. Year 2Levels 13–24

    Development

    More repertoire, new styles, first ensemble playing.

  3. Year 3Levels 25–36

    Refinement

    Advanced technique and expression.

  4. Year 4Levels 37–48

    Mastery

    Solo performance and the graduation recital.

How lessons work

The Wattar method

The same four steps at every level, for every instrument.

  1. 1

    Find your level

    A free first session with a trainer shows where to start.

  2. 2

    Learn in levels

    Four sessions per level, with practice assignments in the student portal.

  3. 3

    Evaluate and move up

    Your trainer evaluates each level before the next one starts.

  4. 4

    Perform

    Concerts, the Wattar Band and the graduation recital.
