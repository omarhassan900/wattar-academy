The path

4 years. 48 levels. One musician.

Each level is four sessions. Your trainer evaluates it before you move on, so parents always know where their child stands.

  1. Year 1Levels 1–12

    Foundation

    Technique, rhythm and reading music.

  2. Year 2Levels 13–24

    Development

    More repertoire, new styles, first ensemble playing.

  3. Year 3Levels 25–36

    Refinement

    Advanced technique and expression.

  4. Year 4Levels 37–48

    Mastery

    Solo performance and the graduation recital.

وتر means string

Every musician starts with one string.

Our mark is the word وتر, cut by channels of equal width. The channels are the strings. Pluck them.
