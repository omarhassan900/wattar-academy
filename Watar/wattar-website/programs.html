4-Year Curriculum

A progressive journey from beginner to advanced musician.

Year 1

Foundation

Months 1–12. Build core skills, learn fundamentals, develop proper technique and music reading.

Year 2

Development

Months 13–24. Expand repertoire, explore styles, and begin ensemble playing.

Year 3

Refinement

Months 25–36. Advanced techniques, performance preparation, and musical expression.

Year 4

Mastery

Months 37–48. Professional-level skills, solo performance, and graduation recital.

Wattar Band Program

More than lessons — a real band experience.

Selected students join the Wattar Band, where they rehearse together, perform live, and experience the collaborative side of music. The band operates in cycles with regular rehearsals and performances.
